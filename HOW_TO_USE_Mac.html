How to use EZR and RStudio (Mac, R 4.5.3, standalone edition)

With these steps, you can use EZR and RStudio inside the browser you usually use (Chrome, Safari and so on). You do not need to install R, RStudio or EZR on your computer, and you install no other app either.

Order of the steps
  1. Check that your Mac can use it (only once, at the start)
  2. Extract the Zip and place the folder
  3. Start with start_mac.command (RStudio opens)
  4. Open EZR
  5. Things to note in the EZR screen
  6. Where data and records are kept
  7. Stop
  8. If something goes wrong

1Check that your Mac can use it (only once, at the start)

Check it in Apple menu → "About This Mac".

ItemWhat is needed

Notes It is safest to have 10 GB or more of free disk space (the EZR and RStudio environment uses about 6 GB, and it is made inside the extracted folder).

2Extract the Zip and place the folder

Double-click the Zip you received (EZR_Standalone_en_r453_….zip), and an EZR_Standalone_en_r453 folder appears in the same place. Put that folder anywhere you like.

When you open the EZR_Standalone_en_r453 folder, you see several files. You use the following.

Places to avoid Avoid folders that are synced by iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive and similar services (reading and writing become slow, and files can conflict). On a Mac, the Desktop and "Documents" are sometimes synced by iCloud Drive (when Finder's sidebar lists Desktop or Documents under iCloud). In that case, put the folder in a place that is not synced (e.g. the "Downloads" folder) and use it there. Files of a few GB holding the environment are made inside this folder.

3Start with start_mac.command

In Finder, double-click start_mac.command in the EZR_Standalone_en_r453 folder. A Terminal window also opens; you can close it.

The first start

If it is blocked with the window below, close it with "Done", and start it with A or B.

A. Start from Terminal
  1. In Finder, choose "Go" → "Utilities" from the menu, and double-click "Terminal" to open it.
  2. In the Terminal window, type bash and one space, drag and drop start_mac.command from Finder into the Terminal window, and press Enter.
B. Press "Open Anyway" in System Settings (your Mac password or Touch ID is needed)
  1. In the Apple menu → "System Settings" → "Privacy & Security", scroll down to "Security".
  2. Press "Open Anyway", and enter your password or use Touch ID. If a confirmation window appears, press "Open Anyway" again (if it does not, double-click start_mac.command again).

Always do the first start from start_mac.command.

What happens when it starts

  1. If you are asked whether to allow access to a folder, check that it is the folder you are using now, and allow it.
  2. The "EZR Standalone" window appears and the preparation starts. The first time, this takes about 10 minutes (you need an internet connection). From the second time on, it takes ten-odd seconds.
  3. When the preparation is finished, RStudio opens in your browser automatically.

Starting from the second time on

If the "EZR Standalone" window is still open, press "Start" in that window. If you closed the window, double-click start_mac.command in Finder (A and B are not needed).

4Open EZR

When RStudio opens, the "Open EZR" window is shown on top of it. You can open EZR with clicks alone.

(1) Choose a project, add data, and press "Open EZR"

The first time, give the new project a name. Choose the data to use (CSV or Excel) with "Browse...", and press "Open EZR". For the name, use ASCII letters and digits and some symbols (_ . -); other characters, such as Japanese, cannot be used. The data can be .csv, .xlsx or .xls files of up to 15 MB each, several at once. If some files cannot be added, the window tells you in red; leave those files out and add the others again.

From the second time on, the projects you made before are listed, most recently used first. To carry on, choose one; to start a different study, choose "+ Create a new project". A project whose folder you deleted is not listed.

(2) EZR opens

EZR opens in a new tab of your browser.

(3) Show the "Open EZR" window again

If you closed the EZR tab, reopened the RStudio tab, or closed the window with "Cancel", press ezr_start.R in "Files" at the bottom right of RStudio to open it, and press "Source". The "Open EZR" window appears again; choose the same project. The EZR that is already open appears as it was. If the window does not appear after "Source" and the Console at the bottom left stays busy (for example after you reopened the RStudio tab), press the red stop button at the top right of the Console, then press "Source" again.

5Things to note in the EZR screen

Write text such as Japanese in the "Text input" box at the bottom of the screen

In the EZR screen, you cannot directly type text that you enter with an input method, such as Japanese. Click the place where you want the text (such as an input field in EZR), then type it in the "Text input" box at the bottom of the screen and press Enter; the text is inserted at that place (at this point, the contents of EZR's clipboard are replaced by the text you typed).

  • Even on a Mac, inside EZR you use the control key, not the command (⌘) key (copy is control+C, paste is control+V).
  • To copy and paste between your computer and EZR, press the small tab at the left edge (③ in the photo of step 4 (2)), and use the clipboard button in the menu that opens.
  • Graphs appear in separate windows. Switch between them with the bar at the bottom of the screen (④ in the same photo).
  • When you close the EZR window, the records of that session are written out and EZR ends. When you open it again with step 4 (3), the records of a new session start.
  • In the "Tools" → "Options..." window, pressing "Restart R Commander" asks "Exit?". Press OK, and if it then asks "Save script file?" and similar questions, press No (the records are kept automatically). The EZR window closes for a moment and opens again with the new settings. The data you read and the records go on as they were (choose the data again with the "Data set:" button).

6Where data and records are kept

In the projects folder of the app folder in the EZR_Standalone_en_r453 folder, a folder is made for each project you make in step 4.

Record fileContents
script.REZR's "R Script" pane
markdown.RmdEZR's "R Markdown" pane
output.txtEZR's "Output" pane
console.logEverything shown by the R that runs EZR
plots folderImages of the graphs (plot_001.png …), saved each time you draw one (adding to a graph rewrites the same image; 3D graphs are not kept)
meta.jsonVersions of R, EZR and the packages, fingerprints (SHA-256) of the files in data (a fingerprint changes if even one character changes, so you can check later whether an analysis used the same data), and the data that were read (at opening and at closing)
markdown.htmlmarkdown.Rmd made into a report with figures, by running the commands again after you close EZR (for analyses that use random numbers, results can differ slightly unless a seed is set; it is made to the end even if an error occurs, and if it could not be made, the reason is written to markdown.log)
  • The records are saved automatically each time a pane changes. You do not need to save.

Reading data in EZR

From EZR's "File" → "Import data", choose the item for your kind of file, and choose the file in the data folder.

  • For a CSV made with Excel on Japanese Windows, choose "CP932 (old Windows)" in "File encode" in the reading window (this keeps Japanese text from being garbled).
  • "Load data set" in the "File" menu is for files in R's format (.RData). Do not use it for CSV or Excel.
  • To read several data sets, change the data set name ("Enter name for data set:") from Dataset from the second one on (with the same name, you are asked whether to overwrite; you can also enter the name through the "Text input" box). Switch the data you use with the "Data set:" button.

7Stop

  1. Press × at the top right of the EZR window, and press OK for "Exit?". If you are then asked, for example, "Save script file?", press "No", because the records are kept automatically.
  2. Press "Stop" in the "EZR Standalone" window.
  3. When it says "Stopped", you may close the "EZR Standalone" window and the browser tabs (if you leave the window open, "Start" starts it again).

"Stop" waits until the records of that session (such as the report markdown.html) are made. If they are not ready yet, it says the following; wait a little and press "Stop" again (with many analyses, this takes a few minutes).

If the same message comes again, the window below appears. Usually press "Wait", wait a little, and press "Stop" again. Only if it still does not stop, press "Stop without waiting" (the report of that session and the like may then be missing).

  • If you try to close the "EZR Standalone" window before stopping, it asks "Stop the environment and quit?". Close EZR first, then press "Stop and quit" (to go back to your work, press "Back").
